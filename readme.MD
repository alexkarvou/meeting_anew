1️⃣ User (or Admin) creates a meeting/team

Can be a casual discussion, business decision, or government policy planning.
Inputs scope & objectives (e.g., "Plan Q2 budget," "Decide next hiring strategy").
Sets constraints & priorities (cost, speed, fairness, risk tolerance).
2️⃣ Stakeholders are invited & profiled

AI gathers info on each participant (past decisions, expertise, preferences).
Uses LLMs + background data to categorize stakeholder influence & biases.
3️⃣ System collects inputs dynamically

Speech-to-text (Whisper-like tech) for verbal input.
Freeform text, documents, structured data (from APIs like company KPIs).
AI summarizes, clusters viewpoints, detects conflicts.
4️⃣ Crafts the dynamic reward function

Reinforcement Learning (RL) or simpler optimizers create a decision model.
Adapts based on meeting goal, participant biases, past outcomes.
Can prioritize consensus, efficiency, innovation, fairness, or risk minimization.
5️⃣ Suggests the "Next Best Policy"

AI proposes one or more optimal decisions, ranked by projected impact.
Uses Monte Carlo simulations & tree search to predict consequences.
If controversial, system explains trade-offs & counterarguments.
6️⃣ Participants can vote or refine

Smart voting (weighting based on expertise, seniority, stake in outcome).
AI refines the model iteratively until consensus is reached (or deadlock is flagged).
7️⃣ Blockchain logs final decision & action items

Ensures transparency & accountability (prevents later disputes).
If needed, smart contracts trigger automated actions (e.g., fund allocation).
